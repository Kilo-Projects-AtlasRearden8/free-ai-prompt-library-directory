# Writing Prompts

Starter prompts for clear, warm writing — blog posts, customer emails, and book blurbs. No account needed; paste into any free AI chat.

---

## Write a friendly blog introduction

**Use case:** You need the opening of a blog post that sounds like a helpful friend, not a textbook. Good for beginner-friendly AI education content.

**Example prompt:**
```
Write a warm, plain-English introduction (3 to 4 short paragraphs) for a blog post about "{{TOPIC}}". The audience is beginners who might feel overwhelmed. Speak like a friend explaining something over coffee: no jargon, no buzzwords, no condescension. End with one sentence that tells the reader what they'll learn. If you use a term that isn't everyday language, explain it in parentheses the first time.
```

**Steps:**
1. Replace `{{TOPIC}}` with your topic (for example, "using AI to draft emails at work").
2. Paste the prompt into any free AI chat (no account needed).
3. Read the draft out loud. If a sentence sounds stiff, ask for a simpler rewrite.
4. Add one personal example or story from your own experience.
5. Copy the result into your blog editor and add your own headline.

## Reply to a customer or client email

**Use case:** You need to answer a customer quickly and politely, without sounding robotic or overly formal.

**Example prompt:**
```
Write a short, friendly email reply to a customer who {{SITUATION}}. Keep it to 3 or 4 short sentences. Tone: warm and helpful, like a small business owner writing to a neighbor. Do not use phrases like "please be advised" or "per my last email." Include a clear next step or offer to help further.
```

**Steps:**
1. Replace `{{SITUATION}}` with the situation (for example, "asked for a refund on a digital guide they already downloaded").
2. Paste into any free AI chat.
3. Check names and order details yourself — never paste real customer names, addresses, or payment info into a public AI chat.
4. Adjust the tone so it sounds like you, then send.

## Write a KDP book blurb

**Use case:** You're publishing on Amazon Kindle Direct Publishing and need a back-cover blurb that hooks readers.

**Example prompt:**
```
Write a Kindle book blurb for "{{BOOK_TITLE}}", a {{GENRE}} book for {{AUDIENCE}}. Structure: a one-line hook, two short paragraphs about the problem and the promise, and one sentence that tells the reader what to do next. Tone: plain English, warm, no hype. Keep the whole blurb under 150 words. Do not mention AI.
```

**Steps:**
1. Fill in `{{BOOK_TITLE}}`, `{{GENRE}}`, and `{{AUDIENCE}}`.
2. Paste into any free AI chat and generate 2 or 3 variations.
3. Pick the one that sounds most like your voice, then paste it into your KDP description.
4. Ask a friend to read it and say whether they'd buy the book based on the blurb alone.
