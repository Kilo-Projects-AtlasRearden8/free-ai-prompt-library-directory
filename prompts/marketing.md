# Marketing Prompts

Starter prompts for small-business marketing copy. Plain, warm, no-hype — like a small business owner writing to a neighbor.

---

## Write a simple marketing email

**Use case:** You want to send one short, friendly email to your audience (customers, readers, or subscribers) without sounding like a billboard.

**Example prompt:**
```
Write a short marketing email for {{PRODUCT_OR_OFFER}} aimed at {{AUDIENCE}}. Structure: a friendly subject line, a 2-3 sentence opening that names the reader's problem, one clear benefit, a single call to action, and a soft sign-off. Tone: warm, plain English, like a small business owner writing to a neighbor. No all-caps, no fake urgency, no emojis. Keep it under 120 words.
```

**Steps:**
1. Fill in `{{PRODUCT_OR_OFFER}}` and `{{AUDIENCE}}`.
2. Paste into any free AI chat.
3. Check the subject line yourself — the best one is often your own.
4. Send from your own email list or platform; never buy a recipient list.
