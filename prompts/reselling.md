# Reselling Prompts

Starter prompts for reselling on eBay, Amazon, and other marketplaces. Listing titles, descriptions, and pricing help — no account needed.

---

## eBay listing title and description

**Use case:** You're listing an item on eBay and want a search-friendly title plus a honest, clear description.

**Example prompt:**
```
Write an eBay listing title (max 80 characters) and a short item description for "{{ITEM}}". Condition: {{CONDITION}}. Include: what the item is, brand and model if known, condition and any flaws, and what ships in the box. Tone: honest and plain-spoken, like you're describing it to a friend. Do not make claims you can't verify. End with shipping and return basics.
```

**Steps:**
1. Replace `{{ITEM}}` with what you're selling (for example, "Nintendo Switch OLED, white").
2. Replace `{{CONDITION}}` with the real condition (for example, "pre-owned, works great, light scratches on the screen").
3. Paste into any free AI chat.
4. Add your own photos and real shipping cost before listing.
5. Never include personal addresses or phone numbers in the prompt or listing.

## Amazon product bullet points

**Use case:** You need benefit-led bullet points for an Amazon product listing.

**Example prompt:**
```
Write five Amazon-style bullet points for "{{PRODUCT}}", a {{CATEGORY}} sold on Amazon. Each bullet: one line, benefit first, feature second. Audience: {{AUDIENCE}}. Tone: clear and confident, no hype, no false claims. Avoid prohibited claims (no medical cures, no "guaranteed" results). Use plain language an eighth-grader could understand.
```

**Steps:**
1. Fill in `{{PRODUCT}}`, `{{CATEGORY}}`, and `{{AUDIENCE}}`.
2. Paste into any free AI chat.
3. Check Amazon's category rules for restricted claims before publishing.
4. Paste the bullets into your Amazon listing and add your own photos.

## Research comparable sold prices

**Use case:** You want to price an item competitively before listing it for resale.

**Example prompt:**
```
I'm reselling {{ITEM}} in {{CONDITION}} condition. Help me plan pricing research: where to look for comparable sold listings (eBay sold items, Amazon, Facebook Marketplace), what details to note (condition, photos, shipping cost, how long it took to sell), and how to set a starting price vs. a firm price. Keep it practical and step by step.
```

**Steps:**
1. Replace `{{ITEM}}` and `{{CONDITION}}` with your item.
2. Paste into any free AI chat.
3. Follow the steps yourself: search sold listings, not active listings.
4. Record prices in a spreadsheet so you can see what actually sells.
