# Fun Extras (Gaming & Chess)

Lightweight prompts for FFXIV and chess hobbies. Just for fun.

---

## FFXIV gathering and crafting plan

**Use case:** You're playing Final Fantasy XIV and want a plan for gathering materials or leveling a craft.

**Example prompt:**
```
I'm playing Final Fantasy XIV and want to gather {{ITEM}} for {{CRAFT/USE}}. My character is level {{LEVEL}} {{CLASS/JOB}}. Suggest a beginner-friendly gathering route: which zones to check, what to look for, and any gear or consumables that help. Keep it short and spoiler-light. If you're not sure about in-game details, say so rather than guessing.
```

**Steps:**
1. Replace item, level, and class/job.
2. Paste into any free AI chat.
3. Verify against an FFXIV database site before spending in-game time.

## Chess opening study plan

**Use case:** You want to learn a chess opening without memorizing 40 moves.

**Example prompt:**
```
I'm a {{RATING}}-rated chess player wanting to learn the {{OPENING}} as {{WHITE/BLACK}}. Suggest a study plan: the first 4-6 moves to memorize, the main ideas (not just move order), one common mistake to avoid, and two free online resources to practice. Keep it under 250 words.
```

**Steps:**
1. Fill in your approximate rating, opening, and color.
2. Paste into any free AI chat.
3. Practice the first few moves on a free chess site before playing them in a real game.
