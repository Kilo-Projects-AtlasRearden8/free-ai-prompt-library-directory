# Healthcare Worker Prompts (Education Only)

Organizational aids for healthcare workers. **These are education and productivity tools only — never medical advice, never a substitute for clinical judgment, and never for patient care decisions.** Do not enter real patient names, IDs, or health information into a public AI chat.

---

## Shift preparation checklist

**Use case:** A nurse or healthcare worker wants a gentle way to organize tasks before and during a shift.

**Example prompt:**
```
Create a short shift-preparation checklist for a healthcare worker starting a {{SHIFT_LENGTH}}-hour {{SHIFT_TYPE}} shift. Include: before you clock in, first 30 minutes on the unit, mid-shift reset, and end-of-shift handoff basics. Keep each item to one line. Tone: calm and practical, not adding stress. Note: this is a general organizational aid, not clinical guidance.
```

**Steps:**
1. Fill in shift length and type.
2. Paste into any free AI chat.
3. Adapt it to your unit's real workflow; follow your facility's policies first.

---

## Plain-language patient education (education only)

**Use case:** You want to practice explaining a common health topic in plain English before speaking with a patient — education and communication practice only.

**Example prompt:**
```
Explain "{{TOPIC}}" in plain English for an adult with no medical background. Use short sentences, everyday words, and an analogy if it helps. Include: what it is, what to watch for, and when to call a clinician. End with: "This is general health information, not medical advice. Ask your care team about your specific situation." Do not give dosing instructions or diagnoses.
```

**Steps:**
1. Replace `{{TOPIC}}` with a general topic (for example, "high blood pressure" or "staying hydrated").
2. Paste into any free AI chat.
3. Review with a clinician before sharing anything with a patient; never use this as medical guidance.
